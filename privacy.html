Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

In short: Atlas has no telemetry, analytics or ads, and runs no servers of its own that collect your data. What Atlas stores stays on your computer.

What stays on your computer

  • Settings, installations and logs are stored in Atlas's data folder on your device.
  • Account sign-in tokens (once sign-in is available) are stored in your operating system's secure credential store, never in plain files. Atlas never sees or stores your Microsoft password.
  • Logs have tokens and secrets removed automatically. You can also hide your username in saved logs (Settings → Privacy).

Network connections Atlas makes

Atlas connects to external services only to do what you ask:

  • Microsoft / Xbox / Minecraft services: to sign you in and confirm you own the game.
  • Mojang's official servers: to download game versions and files.
  • GitHub: to check for and download Atlas updates.
  • Servers you choose: when you check server status or join a server.

These services receive the information any web request includes, such as your IP address, and handle it under their own privacy policies.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no cookies or analytics. GitHub may keep standard server logs; see GitHub's privacy statement. The download page asks GitHub's public API for the latest release.

AI features

Atlas plans an optional local integration for AI assistants. It will be off by default, can be disabled entirely with one switch, and will never give an AI assistant access to your account tokens.

Children

Atlas collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

Updates to this policy are published on this page. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
